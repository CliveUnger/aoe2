Methodology (and its honest limits)

  • Age times are shown as click → arrival (click + research time). Arrival can be later still if villagers were queued ahead of the research.
  • Unit counts are cumulative production (trained over the game), not army size — a big number can be a standing force or waves being replaced. The recorder also duplicates ~2–6% of the recording player's own commands; all counts here are de-duplicated.
  • "Fights" are attrition windows from object-count drops: they include buildings and expiring farms, and long windows are campaigns, not single battles. Windows ending at the game's end (surrender wipe) are shown but excluded from records.
  • Cancelled-then-reclicked research is collapsed to the final click. Cost/upgrade facts checked against current game data (July 2026 patch).
